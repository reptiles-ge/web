overview: >-
  The collared dwarf snake (Eirenis collaris) is a small, harmless colubrid.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 treat it as a biologically confirmed species with an
  Eastern Mediterranean/Caucasus range. In Georgia it is recorded in the
  southeast. The subspecies is E. c. collaris. A paper figure locality is
  Taribana, Dedoplistskaro district. It is often confused with the ring-headed
  dwarf snake (Eirenis modestus).
