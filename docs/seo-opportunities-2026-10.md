## Executive finding

In Georgian Google, the competitor for animal and pest queries is Facebook, not other websites. On almost every results page inspected, the top ten were Facebook groups, YouTube/TikTok clips, 2012–2019 news rewrites, WordPress/Blogspot posts and Wikipedia. reptiles.ge is already on page one for "საშიში ცხოველები საქართველოში" (via the mammals hub) and "გველი სახლში" (via the yard guide) with pages that were never written for those queries.

Three consequences:

1. **Problem queries beat encyclopedic ones.** "X სახლში / ეზოში / ნაკბენი / როგორ მოვიშოროთ" are answered by Facebook comment threads with 100–1,000+ comments. The guide-article format is the right weapon.
2. **Every page is four opportunities.** Russian and English searches made in Georgia ("змеи в грузии", "медузы в батуми опасны или нет", "snakes in georgia country") have results as weak as the Georgian ones, and the site already ships KA/EN/RU/TR.
3. **The window is closing in pest topics.** exterminator.ge (first indexed July–September 2026) already ranks #1 for "ბზიკი და კრაზანა" and appears for rats. Wildlife topics are still uncontested.

## How Georgians actually search

- **Homonym traps.** Many animal words are dominated by other meanings: "მორიელი" (zodiac), "გველის პერანგი" (the novel), "ჩიტების გამომზამთრებელი" (school story), "ობობა" (tow trucks), "სალამანდრა" (shoe shop), "კრაზანა" (the herb). Always read the results page before trusting a seed.
- **Dream intent** is the largest animal modifier ("გველი სიზმარში", "თაგვი სიზმარში"). Real demand, but vanity traffic for a scientific atlas. Rejected.
- **Omen intent** ("ღამურა სახლში რას ნიშნავს", "ბუ რისი ნიშანია") sits next to practical intent. Google mixes horoscope sites with how-to pages, and the bat guide already ranks there.
- **Translation and homework modifiers** attach to almost every animal: "ინგლისურად", "რუსულად", "ინფორმაცია", "საინტერესო ფაქტები", "ფოტო", "რას ჭამს", "ხმა".
- **Colloquial beats formal:** "ანკარა გველი", "წყლის გველი", "შავი გველი საქართველოში", "ბზიკი" used interchangeably with "კრაზანა", "ნაკმენი" as a common misspelling of "ნაკბენი".
- **School-phrased list queries:** "საქართველოში გავრცელებული ძუძუმწოვარი ცხოველები / ამფიბიები / ქვეწარმავლები / ენდემური სახეობები".

### Rejected

- **Dream interpretation:** wrong audience.
- **"ჩიტების გამომზამთრებელი":** a school text, not a bird query.
- **Rabies and dog bites:** medical publishers own them, and they sit badly with the no-medical-protocol rule.
- **"კოღოს / ბუზების საწინააღმდეგო", "თაგვის ხაფანგი":** shopping intent.
- **"ნადირობის სეზონი":** half the results are a film, and it is a brand conflict for a wildlife atlas.

## The Georgia SEO opportunity formula

```text
Real Opportunity = (Demand signals × Social share of page one × Intent fit × Query surface × Locale multiplier × Timing)
                   ÷ (Production cost × Integrity risk)
```

- **Demand signals:** the count of independent sources, not volume. Tool volume is unreliable in a market this small.
- **Social share of page one:** the best single predictor found. Facebook and video results mean Google has nothing better.
- **Intent fit:** problem queries reward a structured answer; dream and shopping queries do not.
- **Query surface:** how many matrix patterns the seed supports.
- **Locale multiplier:** whether Russian and English autocomplete show the same query. Free for us, and competitors rarely have it.
- **Timing:** months until peak; four to six is ideal.
- **Production cost:** audio, photos and new sourcing cost more than text.
- **Integrity risk:** specific to this site. A page needing unsourced claims costs more than it earns.

### Worksheet

Score each 0–3; multiply the top six, divide by the product of the last two.

| Variable                  | 0                 | 1                         | 2                                 | 3                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Demand signals            | Autocomplete only | + related searches        | + Facebook thread of 50+ comments | + Search Console impressions or news recurrence |
| Social share of page one  | 0–1 results       | 2–3                       | 4–5                               | 6+                                              |
| Intent fit                | Other meaning     | Info only                 | Problem query                     | Problem query, no guide exists                  |
| Query surface             | 1 pattern         | 2–3                       | 4–6                               | 7+                                              |
| Locale multiplier         | KA only           | —                         | KA + RU or EN                     | KA + RU + EN                                    |
| Timing                    | Peak passed       | Flat                      | Peak in 1–3 months                | Peak in 4–6 months                              |

Build anything at 100 or above. Treat 30–100 as an expansion of an existing page. Drop the rest.
