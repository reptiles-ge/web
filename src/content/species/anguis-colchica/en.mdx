overview: >-
  The Colchis slow worm (Anguis colchica) is a legless anguid lizard.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 treat it as a candidate species: it belongs to the
  widespread western Eurasian A. fragilis complex; Gvoždík et al. (2010)
  elevated the Caucasian form (A. f. colchica) to species rank. It is likely
  allopatric with A. graeca and A. fragilis; cytochrome b difference from those
  relatives is 6.5–8%. That does not confirm species rank in this atlas — it
  remains a candidate. It is often confused with the European glass lizard or
  with snakes. The checklist figure is from Nigoeti; barcodes are from
  Mtisdziri environs, Baniskhevi, and Lanchkhuti. It is harmless to people.

  - question: How does it differ from the glass lizard?
    answer: >-

      with a clear side fold and often a yellow belly. The slow worm is smaller
      and smoother. Both are harmless.
