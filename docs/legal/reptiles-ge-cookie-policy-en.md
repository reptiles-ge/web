# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Reptiles.ge uses cookies and similar browser storage. It complements the [Privacy Policy](route:/privacy).

## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text record stored by your browser. It can remember a technical preference, such as your language or consent choice, or help us understand how the site is used.

## Cookies Reptiles.ge uses

Reptiles.ge uses only necessary and analytics categories. Necessary cookies are required for the site to work. Analytics cookies are used only when you give consent.

| Cookie                    | Provider         | Purpose                                                                       | Duration                | Category  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------- |
| `reptiles_cookie_consent` | Reptiles.ge      | Stores your cookie preferences.                                               | Approximately 12 months | Necessary |
| `rp_v`                    | Reptiles.ge      | Limits the first-party visit notification after analytics consent is granted. | Approximately 24 hours  | Analytics |
| `_ga`                     | Google Analytics | Helps Google Analytics measure visits and site usage.                         | Typically up to 2 years | Analytics |
| `_ga_*`                   | Google Analytics | Stores Google Analytics measurement/session data for a specific property.     | Typically up to 2 years | Analytics |

## Necessary cookies

Necessary cookies store your cookie preference and support technical site functionality. This category is always enabled because we need it to remember the choice you made.

## Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand which pages are used, which languages are active, and how search, quizzes, galleries, and other features perform. We use this information to improve the site and its content.

## Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager

The site loads Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics only after Analytics consent is granted. Before consent, Google Consent Mode v2 defaults are denied: `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` are all denied.

If you accept Analytics, only `analytics_storage` changes to granted. Advertising-related consent remains denied.

## Top.ge and first-party visit notification

After Analytics consent is granted, the site may load the Top.ge counter and send a first-party visit notification to Reptiles.ge. These are not used to block the main site content or restrict navigation.

## Managing consent

On your first visit, the site loads normally and a small cookie banner appears near the bottom of the page. You can choose necessary cookies only, open settings, or accept Analytics.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie Settings button in the footer.

## Withdrawing consent

If you withdraw Analytics consent, Reptiles.ge updates Google Consent Mode back to denied, stops future analytics events, and removes Google Analytics cookies owned by this domain where reasonably possible, including `_ga` and `_ga_*`.

## Third-party processing

When Analytics consent is granted, data may be processed by Google and Top.ge under their own terms and retention policies. Hosting and CDN infrastructure is also used for technical delivery, as described in the [Privacy Policy](route:/privacy).

## Policy updates

This policy may be updated if cookies, analytics configuration, or legal requirements change. When that happens, the date above will be updated.
