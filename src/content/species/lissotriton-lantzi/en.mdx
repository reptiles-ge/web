overview: >-
  The Caucasian smooth newt (Lissotriton lantzi) is a candidate species in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026: a geographically isolated evolutionary lineage
  within the L. vulgaris complex. Pabijan et al. (2015) treat it as the oldest
  isolated lineage in the complex (late Miocene); isolation time (~6.4 million
  years) may not be enough for postzygotic isolation. Frost (2026) lists it as
  a separate species; Raffaëlli (2022) does not. A barcoded specimen is from
  Mtskemsi Lake, Tbilisi; Bakuriani is mentioned as homologous. The checklist
  figure is Tabaruki / Mtskemsi.
