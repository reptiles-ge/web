overview: >-
  The Caucasian rock lizard (Darevskia caucasica) is biologically confirmed in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026. Doronin et al. (2021) report strong genetic
  differences between Georgian populations and those of the northern Caucasus;
  a genomic study (Tarkhnishvili et al. 2025) supports several genetically
  distant units within D. caucasica. It forms a narrow contact zone with D. mixta
  in the Greater Caucasus. The discussion notes contiguous ranges with
  D. daghestanica in the upper Tergi (Terek), where they may hybridize. Barcodes:
  Zakagori Fortress, Mount Tbatana, Lebarde; figure: Truso Valley, Zakagori.
