overview: >-
  Schneider’s skink (Eumeces schneiderii) is a scincid lizard. Tarkhnishvili et
  al. 2026 list it as biologically confirmed: the species occurs in West Asia
  and North Africa; subspecies E. s. princeps is the Caucasus/Iran form. In
  Georgia it is found in the extreme southeast. princeps is sometimes treated
  at species rank as Novoeumeces princeps — the checklist keeps it as a
  subspecies. The figure is from Vashlovani National Park; barcodes are from
  Chachuna Managed Reserve. It is harmless to people.
habitat: >-

  Park; barcoded specimens are from Chachuna Managed Reserve.
diet: >-
  Small invertebrates; the checklist does not specify diet.
behavior: >-
  A diurnal skink with smooth, shiny scales. Do not confuse it with the tiny

  here.
