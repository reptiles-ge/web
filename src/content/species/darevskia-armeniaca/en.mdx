Its main locations are Lake Saghamo in Ninotsminda Municipality and Akhalkalaki. A 2026 study lists it as a separate species rather than a possible one. A similarly coloured lizard in these places is not always D. armeniaca, because Darevskia species often look alike. This lizard's origins are linked to D. valentini and D. mixta, while its DNA line passed down from the mother also matches D. mixta and D. dahli. These links offer no simple rule for identification: colour alone, a single photo or one feature is often not enough. To identify it reliably, consider where you saw the lizard, take a detailed photo and check the features of its scales.
