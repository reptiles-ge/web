overview: >-
  The Caucasian green lizard (Lacerta media) is a lacertid. Tarkhnishvili et
  al. 2026 treat it as a candidate species: it belongs to the monophyletic
  L. trilineata complex; separate status is indirectly supported by phylogeny
  and parapatry in western Anatolia, but the contact zone with the L.
  trilineata group remains unexplored (Ahmadzadeh et al. 2013). Cytochrome b
  difference from L. trilineata reaches 6.5%. That does not confirm species
  rank in this atlas. It occurs in central and eastern Anatolia, Iran, and the
  Caucasus; in Georgia it is widespread in semiarid habitats of the east and
  sporadic in the northwest. The figure is from Tmogvi village; barcodes are
  from Pantishara Gorge and Tmogvi. It is harmless to people.
