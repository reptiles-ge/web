description: >-
  Georgia’s only land tortoise — a high-domed shell, not a pond turtle.
overview: >-
  The Mediterranean tortoise (Testudo graeca) is Georgia’s only land tortoise.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 list it as biologically confirmed. T. g. ibera is
  widespread in eastern Georgia; T. g. nikolskii was described from western
  Georgia and is considered rare. The checklist keeps nikolskii for phenotypic
  differences and a distinct range; genetic distinction between the subspecies
  is not yet demonstrated. A barcoded specimen is from Tbilisi.
habitat: >-
  Open, dry terrestrial landscapes. In the east — T. g. ibera; in the west
  T. g. nikolskii is rare. The Tbilisi specimen is a locality, not a full-country
  map.
diet: >-
  Mostly herbivorous — grasses and herbaceous plants.
behavior: >-
  Terrestrial; active in warm weather. It does not swim like Georgia’s
  freshwater turtles.
conservation: >-
  A native, biologically confirmed species. T. g. nikolskii is considered rare.
  This profile does not cite a national Red List entry.
