overview: >-
  The snake-eyed lizard (Ophisops elegans) is a lacertid with fused eyelids.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 list it as biologically confirmed, with a broad
  western Asian range. It is found sporadically in southeast Georgia. The
  subspecies is O. e. elegans. NCBI GenBank sequences are highly divergent —
  taxonomy of O. elegans sensu lato may be revised. Figure and barcodes are
  from Taribana, Dedoplistskaro district. It is harmless to people.
