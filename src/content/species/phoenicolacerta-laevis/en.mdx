overview: >-
  The Lebanon lizard (Phoenicolacerta laevis) is a lacertid. Tarkhnishvili et
  al. 2026 treat species status as biologically valid and list it as
  unintentionally introduced: a single location in Georgia, genetically close
  to populations from southern Türkiye. The Georgian record is Tarkhnishvili
  et al. (2017a); the discussion names it, with Trachemys scripta, as
  introduced from distant geographic regions (Tarkhnishvili et al. 2017b;
  Reshetnikov et al. 2023). Figure and barcodes are from Anaklia. It is
  harmless to people.
habitat: >-
