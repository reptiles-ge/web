overview: >-
  The sand lizard (Lacerta agilis) is a lacertid. Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026
  list it as biologically confirmed, with a broad western Eurasian range. It
  occurs everywhere in western Georgia; in eastern Georgia above 600–800 m.
  The checklist names three subspecies: L. a. ioriensis (eastern Georgia),
  L. a. grusinica (western Georgia), L. a. brevicaudata (southern and central
  Georgia). The figure is from Ivlita village; barcodes are from Tskruti and
  Tianeti. It is harmless to people.
habitat: >-
