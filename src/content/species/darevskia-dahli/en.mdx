overview: >-
  Dahl’s rock lizard (Darevskia dahli) is a parthenogenetic hybrid form in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026. The parental species are D. mixta and
  D. portschinskii. The mitochondrial genome is shared with D. mixta and
  parthenogenetic D. armeniaca — so high mtDNA similarity among the three is
  expected and is not a field colour mark. Barcode and figure are from Kojori.
  This is not D. armeniaca: that parthenogen’s parents are valentini and mixta,
  with a southern Georgian range.
