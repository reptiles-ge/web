overview: >-
  The striped lizard (Lacerta strigata) is a lacertid. Tarkhnishvili et al.
  2026 list it as biologically confirmed: West Asia and the Caucasus,
  separated from closest relatives >10 Mya (Kumar et al. 2017). In Georgia it
  is found in semiarid habitats throughout the country. The figure is a
  juvenile from Dedoplistskaro district, Chankaani; the barcode is from
  Chankaani. It is harmless to people.
