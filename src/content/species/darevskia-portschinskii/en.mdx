overview: >-
  Portschinsky’s lizard (Darevskia portschinskii) is biologically confirmed in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026. It is closely related to D. rudis, D. obscura, and
  D. valentini, but is substantially smaller and forms a narrow contact zone
  with all three. A hybrid zone with D. obscura is present in the Tana River
  valley. It is endemic to the central Lesser Caucasus. A barcode is from
  Kojori — dahli is also barcoded there; that is a different taxon. Subspecies:
  D. p. portschinskii and D. p. nigrita.
