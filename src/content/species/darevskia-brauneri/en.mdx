overview: >-
  Brauner's Rock Lizard (Darevskia brauneri) is not venomous and is associated with the western and central Greater Caucasus. Places in Georgia mentioned in connection with it include Mestia, Martvili and Ertso Lake. It belongs to the D. saxicola group and is widely accepted as a separate species. However, Georgia's 2026 checklist still leaves this question open. It has not yet been fully checked where the boundaries lie between this lizard, D. saxicola and D. szczerbaki, or where they meet. Nor has it been fully checked how their features change gradually from place to place. The D. arribasi described near Ertso is not treated as a separate species and remains a subspecies, D. b. arribasi. Colour or a single photo is not enough to call a similar-looking lizard Darevskia brauneri.
