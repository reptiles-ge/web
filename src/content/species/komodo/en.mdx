overview: >-
  The Komodo dragon is the largest living lizard on Earth. It lives on a handful
  of Indonesian islands and is known for its strength, patience, and venomous
  bite.
habitat: >-
  Distributed on the islands of Komodo, Rinca, Flores, and Gili Motang. It lives
  in dry forests, savanna, and coastal lowlands.
diet: >-
  It feeds on deer, wild boar, birds, and carrion. Juveniles often live in trees
  and feed on insects.
behavior: >-
  It is a solitary predator. It hunts by ambush and can wait for prey for hours.
  It is territorial, especially during the breeding season.
