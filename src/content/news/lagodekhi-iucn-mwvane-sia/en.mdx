## What is happening in Lagodekhi?

For one week, Lagodekhi Protected Areas are hosting the steering committee of the IUCN World Commission on Protected Areas (WCPA). About 30 international experts and commission members are taking part. At the opening, Tamar Khakhishvili, deputy chair of the Agency of Protected Areas, presented Georgia’s protected-area system and how it is managed, with particular attention to Lagodekhi’s international importance.

The WCPA is a global network of experts on protected and conserved areas. It has more than 3,200 members from about 140 countries. The commission advises policymakers and helps build capacity and investment for protected areas. A meeting like this shows the experts Georgia’s management practice on the ground and gives state agencies a chance to present their view of the long-term development of the protected-area system.

## Two candidacies

Lagodekhi was founded in 1912 and is Georgia’s oldest protected area. It is now a candidate for two international awards.

The IUCN Green List. This IUCN programme assesses effective and inclusive conservation in protected and conserved areas against a global standard. Lagodekhi is still at the candidate stage.

The European Diploma. The Council of Europe has awarded it since 1965 to natural and semi-natural areas of European importance. The diploma is granted for five years and can be renewed.

## Lagodekhi in this atlas

Lagodekhi is in <Region id="kakheti">Kakheti</Region> and appears on many atlas pages, as a range locality or as the place a photo was taken.

The high mountains are home to the <Species id="capra-cylindricornis">East Caucasian tur</Species>. The photos on its profile, including this article’s main image, were taken in Lagodekhi. So were a <Species id="cervus-elaphus">red deer</Species> fawn and, in April 2026, a <Species id="gypaetus-barbatus">bearded vulture</Species>.

Among amphibians, the <Species id="ommatotriton-ophryticus">northern banded newt</Species> occurs sporadically in eastern Georgia, as far as Telavi and Lagodekhi, and lives in the Lagodekhi protected area. Georgian localities of the <Species id="pelodytes-caucasicus">Caucasian parsley frog</Species> reach along the southern slope of the Caucasus from Barisakho to Lagodekhi. Among reptiles, public observations of the <Species id="elaphe-urartica">Urartian ratsnake</Species> include Lagodekhi Municipality.
