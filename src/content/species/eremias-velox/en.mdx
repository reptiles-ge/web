overview: >-
  The rapid racerunner (Eremias velox) is a lacertid. Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026
  list it as biologically confirmed, with a broad range in the Caucasus and
  Central Asia. It is fairly common in southeast Georgia. The subspecies is
  E. v. caucasia; COI differences suggest caucasica may be a species separate
  from E. velox — infraspecific taxonomy needs population-level study. The
  figure is from Vashlovani National Park; barcodes are from Pantishara Canyon.
  It is harmless to people.
