overview: >-
  The eastern treefrog (Hyla orientalis) is a candidate species in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026. Separate species status has been supported mainly
  by mitochondrial phylogenetic differentiation (Stöck et al. 2008); the
  contact zone with nominal H. arborea s.s. has not been studied enough with
  multilocus genomic data, so reproductive isolation is not fully shown. The
  checklist lists it as a distinct species following most recent treatments,
  but the status needs integrative validation. It is parapatric with H.
  savignyi in eastern Georgia; gene flow across that contact zone is not fully
  resolved. The checklist figure is Gaghma Dvabzu.
