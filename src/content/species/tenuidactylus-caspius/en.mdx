overview: >-
  The Caspian gecko (Tenuidactylus caspius) is a gekkonid lizard. Tarkhnishvili
  et al. 2026 list it as biologically confirmed; the subspecies is T. c.
  caspius. Its natural range is the extreme southeast of Georgia. The same
  paper’s discussion: it is native there and forms exclave populations in
  Georgian cities, including those on the Black Sea coast (Prondzynska 2025).
  Barcoded specimens are from Tbilisi and Samukhi valley; the figure is
  Dedoplistskaro district, Samukhi Valley. It is harmless to people.
habitat: >-
  Natural range in the extreme southeast; also urban settlements, including

  (Dedoplistskaro).
diet: >-

behavior: >-
