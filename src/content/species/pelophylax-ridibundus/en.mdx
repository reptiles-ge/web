overview: >-
  The marsh frog (Pelophylax ridibundus) is biologically confirmed. Parts of
  the literature treat P. bedriagae at species rank; Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026
  treat bedriagae as a junior synonym of P. ridibundus or a geographic
  subspecies (Tarkhnishvili et al. 2024): COI from Georgia is closer to Levant
  P. bedriagae s.l. than to European P. ridibundus (difference over 5%), but
  both haplogroups occur in the type area of the species. Barcodes are
  throughout Georgia; point localities are in Tarkhnishvili et al. 2024. The
  checklist figure is Tsalenjikha, Skuri.
