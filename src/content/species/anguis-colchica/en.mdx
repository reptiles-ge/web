description: >-
  The Colchis slow worm (Anguis colchica) is a legless lizard recorded in
  Georgia. It is harmless to people and is neither a snake nor the European
  glass lizard. It remains a candidate species in the checklist.
overview: >-
  The Colchis slow worm (Anguis colchica) is a legless anguid lizard. In Georgia
  it is often mistaken for a snake or the European glass lizard, although it is
  harmless to people. Tarkhnishvili et al. (2026) treat it as a candidate
  species in the widespread western Eurasian A. fragilis complex. Gvoždík et al.
  (2010) raised the Caucasian form (A. f. colchica) to species rank, but the
  checklist does not treat that rank as settled. According to the checklist, the
  Colchis slow worm probably occurs apart from A. graeca and A. fragilis. Its
  cytochrome b DNA sequence differs from those relatives by 6.5–8%. The
  checklist figure comes from Nigoeti; DNA barcodes come from the Mtisdziri
  environs, Baniskhevi and Lanchkhuti.
habitat: >-
  The Georgian places named in the sources are the village of Nigoeti, the
  Mtisdziri environs, Baniskhevi and Lanchkhuti. The gallery also contains
  photos taken in Borjomi-Kharagauli National Park. These places do not describe
  the full range. The checklist does not specify a habitat type, so this profile
  does not present forest, grassland or mountain as confirmed habitat
  requirements. The map shades only regions marked confirmed in its
  records-by-region table; an individual record alone does not establish
  distribution across a region. If you see a small, smooth, snake-like legless
  lizard in Georgia, check its body features as well as the surroundings.
diet: Small invertebrates; the checklist does not specify diet.
behavior: >-
  A secretive legless lizard. It is not a snake: it has movable eyelids. Smaller
  and smoother than the glass lizard, without a clear lateral fold.
conservation: >-

    eyelids. It is [smaller and smoother than the glass

  traits:
    - >-
      Its body looks snake-like, but it is a legless lizard with movable
      eyelids. Snakes do not have movable eyelids. It is secretive.
    - >-
      It is smaller and smoother than [the glass lizard](pseudopus-apodus); it
      has no clear fold or groove along the side.
    - >-
      The glass lizard (Pseudopus apodus) often has a yellow belly and hind-limb
      rudiments near the cloaca. The slow worm is not read by those marks.
    - Harmless to people; neither the slow worm nor the glass lizard is a snake.
    - >-
      Often confused with the glass lizard or with snakes. In this atlas it
      remains a candidate in the A. fragilis complex — species rank is not
      treated as settled here.
  coloration: >-
    Some described A. colchica have blue spots on the back. Published

faq:

    answer: 'No — it is a legless lizard. Unlike a snake, it has movable eyelids.'
  - question: How does it differ from the glass lizard?
    answer: >-
      The European glass lizard (Pseudopus apodus) is a large legless lizard
      with a clear side fold and often a yellow belly. The slow worm is smaller
      and smoother. Both are harmless.
