commonName: Griffon vulture
location: Year-round resident and migrant in Georgia
description: >-
  A large pale-bodied vulture with dark flight feathers and a short tail. Confirmed
  in Georgia as a breeding resident and a migrant on the 2024 national bird
  checklist. Named colonies are documented in Vashlovani and Kazbegi.
overview: >-
  Gyps fulvus is a large bird that feeds on carrion and nests in groups on cliffs. It nests in Georgia and is found there year-round. Migrating birds are also seen there. It has a light brown body, dark feathers on its wings and a short tail. An ordinary encounter poses no danger to people, but keep away from its nests and the places where these birds nest in groups. Globally, it faces a low risk of extinction, but Georgia’s current Red List considers it at risk of extinction.
habitat: >-
  In Georgia, groups of orbi nests have been recorded in the Vashlovani Protected Areas in Kakheti and Kazbegi National Park in Mtskheta-Mtianeti. The confirmed sites in Vashlovani are Eagle Gorge and the Khornabuji cliffs. In Kazbegi, they are Dariali Gorge near Gveleti and the area around Mount Kabarjini. Orbi nest on cliffs and look for food in open areas. In the Caucasus, they occupied nesting cliffs more often where rainfall was lower, there was more open space, and more dead livestock were found nearby. The distribution map confirms the species in Mtskheta — Mtianeti, Kakheti, Kvemo Kartli, Samegrelo — Zemo Svaneti and Samtskhe — Javakheti; these are observations and do not confirm nesting, which is documented only at the sites named above. Orbi have also been recorded migrating near Batumi in autumn; this does not mean they nest there.
diet: >-
  Orbi mainly feeds on the carcasses of large domestic and wild mammals. It does not usually hunt large live animals. Poisoned carcasses endanger orbi and other animals that eat them. Do not lure the bird with food.
behavior: >-
  It is active during the day and nests in small groups on cliffs. Adults stay at these nesting sites year-round, while young birds mostly head south after breeding and return in spring. It mates mostly in December–January and usually lays one egg between February and April. Both parents sit on the egg for 48–65 days. The chick leaves the nest 97–136 days after hatching. It rarely makes a sound in flight; near a carcass or the nest, it hisses and makes low calls. Do not approach these nesting sites.
conservation: >-
  A 2021 global assessment found that the risk of extinction for the griffon vulture is low. Under Georgia’s current 2014 Red List, the bird faces a risk of extinction in the country because its population there is small. A 2024 bird list gives it the national category NT, but that list is based on an update that has not yet been officially approved and does not replace the current Red List. An estimated 120–1,300 pairs nested in Georgia in 2013–2017. This wide range is based on limited data, not a complete count. Poisoning and disturbance of nests may threaten these birds. Do not catch or kill them.
interaction: >-
  An ordinary encounter with a griffon vulture is not dangerous to people, but do not touch the bird. Keep away from its nest and places where these birds nest together. The griffon vulture is on Georgia’s Red List; do not catch or kill it. Poisoned bait is dangerous to scavengers.

  traits:
    - >-
      Its body is about 93–122 cm long, with a wingspan of 2.3–2.8 m. Its head and neck are covered in short down; adults have a pale collar. The body is pale sandy brown, while the flight feathers and tail feathers are dark. In flight, its long, broad wings with splayed tips and short tail are visible. It often holds its wings in a slight V shape. Males and females look alike, though females are usually larger.
    - >-
      A young one has a browner collar and a more mottled back. It develops adult coloration over several years.
    - >-
      [Aegypius monachus](aegypius-monachus) is uniformly dark. It has a short tail that narrows toward the tip and often holds its wings flatter in flight. [Gypaetus barbatus](gypaetus-barbatus) has narrow wings, a long diamond-shaped tail and a rust-colored body. Adults also have a black “beard.” Neophron percnopterus is much smaller. Adults are white, with black feathers on their wings and a yellow face. [Aquila chrysaetos](aquila-chrysaetos) has a long tail, a golden nape and feathered legs; it hunts live prey.
    - >-
      A large bird seen near a group of birds on a cliff, with a pale body and dark feathers on its wings, is more likely a vulture than an eagle. Do not approach the group.
