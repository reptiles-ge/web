overview: >-
  The Transcaucasian ratsnake (Zamenis hohenackeri) is a harmless colubrid.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 treat it as a biologically confirmed species. The
  subspecies in Georgia is Z. h. hohenackeri. It is genetically close to other
  populations from the Caucasus and eastern Türkiye. A paper figure locality is
  Gomaro Village, Adigeni district. It is often confused with the Aesculapian
  snake (Zamenis longissimus).
