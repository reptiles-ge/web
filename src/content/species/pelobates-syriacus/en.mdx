  The eastern spadefoot (Pelobates syriacus) is biologically confirmed. It is
  parapatric with its closest relative, P. fuscus, without signs of
  hybridization. Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026: found in eastern Georgia. Spadefoots
  from the Balkans traditionally attributed to P. syriacus may belong to P.
  balcanicus; COI differences between Georgia and the Balkans exceed 10%.
  Subspecies concept P. s. boettgeri (Dufresnes et al. 2019); Georgian
  populations are tentatively assigned by regional affiliation. The barcode is
  from Chachuna. The checklist figure is Tbilisi.
