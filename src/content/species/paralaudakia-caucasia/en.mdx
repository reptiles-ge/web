overview: >-
  Paralaudakia caucasia is the only lizard from the agama family in Georgia. It is not venomous to humans. Its presence is confirmed in Kakheti, Shida Kartli, Kvemo Kartli, Tbilisi, Mtskheta-Mtianeti and Samtskhe-Javakheti. There are only isolated records from Imereti and Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti; these regions are not considered part of its range. It has a flattened body and spines arranged in rings on its tail. It is active during the day.
habitat: >-
  Jojo lives on dry, rocky slopes and steep cliffs. Its flattened body helps it slip into narrow crevices. It also shelters under rocks and in burrows. In Georgia, Jojo has been found in Fia and Tmogvi in Aspindza Municipality, and around Datviskhevi and Pantishara in Dedoplistskaro Municipality. It is also found on the Iori Plateau, in Kartli, Borjomi Municipality and Meskheti.

  summary: >-
    The jojo is a sturdy lizard with a flattened body, a triangular head and a spiny tail. The scales on its tail form distinct rings. In a photo taken from a distance, a small jojo and [Tenuidactylus caspius](tenuidactylus-caspius) may be confused with each other if the eye and tail scales are not clearly visible.
  traits:
    - >-
      It has a flattened, triangular head. The ear opening is clearly visible.
    - >-
      The scales on the back are not all alike: they are relatively large along the spine and more spiny on the sides of the body.
    - >-
      The tail is about twice as long as the body and is covered in rings of spiny scales. This is one of the most noticeable features for identifying the animal.
    - >-
      The back is usually olive-colored or gray. The body may have yellowish spots or a dark, net-like pattern. During the breeding season, the male’s chest and the sides of its belly may darken.
    - >-
      A young jojo may have dark spots or crosswise stripes on its back; the Caspian gecko has a similar pattern. Up close, look at the gecko’s vertical pupil and the rings of spines on the jojo’s tail. Do not tell them apart only by whether you saw them by day or at night: the gecko is mostly active at night, but it has also been seen during the day.
