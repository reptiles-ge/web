overview: >-
  Brauner’s rock lizard (Darevskia brauneri) is a candidate species in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026, in the Darevskia (saxicola) complex. Species status
  is widely accepted, but it is unclear how far stepped genetic and phenotypic
  clines separate it from D. saxicola or D. szczerbaki. Mitochondrial haplogroups
  from some Georgian populations of nominal D. brauneri are closer to North
  Caucasian D. saxicola than to formally conspecific populations. Distribution
  is restricted to the western and central Greater Caucasus. D. arribasi from
  near Ertso Lake is treated here as D. b. arribasi: diagnostic scalation and
  morphometrics fall within variation of western Caucasian brauneri and of
  Enguri and Tekhuri basin populations, and mtDNA divergence is on a ≈2% scale.
  Subspecies D. b. myusserica occurs locally in northwestern Georgia.
