overview: >-
  The Ajarian rock lizard (Darevskia adjarica) is a candidate species in
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026: it was formerly treated as a geographic subspecies
  of D. parvula. The range extends over southwestern Georgia. Cytochrome b
  difference between Georgian D. ajarica and D. parvula from northeastern Turkey
  reaches 3.7%; the transition zone of the D. parvula complex has not been
  studied at population level, so free gene flow cannot yet be rejected.
  Barcoded specimens are from Shuakhevi; the closest homologous sequence is from
  Ajaristskali (deposited as D. parvula).
