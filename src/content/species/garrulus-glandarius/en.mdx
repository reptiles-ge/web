commonName: Eurasian jay
location: Year-round resident and breeder in Georgia
description: >-
  A medium-sized woodland corvid with a black crown in the Caucasus form and a
  bright blue wing panel. Confirmed in Georgia as a year-round resident and
  breeder on the 2024 national bird checklist.
overview: >-
  Garrulus glandarius is a bird related to crows and ravens. It lives in Georgia year-round and nests here. You can recognize it by the blue patch with black stripes on its wing, its black “moustache” and the white feathers above its tail. It is not dangerous to people. It is also called japara in Georgian. The Caucasian form found in Georgia has mostly black feathers on its head.
habitat: >-
  ჩხიკვი is a forest bird. It is found in deciduous and mixed forests, especially oak forests, as well as at forest edges, in parks and in settlements with trees. Its presence in Georgia has been confirmed, including in Algeti National Park and Tbilisi. Treeless high mountain areas are not its typical habitat.
diet: >-
  ჩხიკვი eats acorns, other seeds, fruit and insects, and sometimes the eggs and chicks of small birds. In autumn, it hides acorns in the ground and among fallen leaves, then later finds some of them. The acorns left behind may sprout, helping oak trees spread.
behavior: >-
  This bird is active during the day. It has a loud, harsh call and also imitates other birds’ calls. In flight, the white area above its tail and the blue patch on its wing are clearly visible. It builds its nest in a tree or a tall bush.
conservation: >-
  A 2017 global assessment found that ჩხიკვი is at relatively low risk. The 2024 list of Georgia’s birds gives the same assessment for Georgia. This does not establish that the bird is protected by law.
interaction: >-
  This bird is not dangerous to people. Do not catch wild birds.

    - >-
      It measures about 35 cm from its beak to the end of its tail. Its wingspan is 52–58 cm, and it weighs about 140–190 g. Its body is pinkish brown, with a black “moustache” near the throat. In flight, a white area above the tail and a blue wing patch with black bars are visible. When the wing is clearly visible, this patch is the most useful feature for identifying it.
    - >-
