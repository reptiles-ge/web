description: >-
  A worm-like harmless blind snake; biologically confirmed in the Georgia checklist.
overview: >-
  The European blind snake (Xerotyphlops vermicularis) is a harmless, fossorial
  typhlopid. Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 treat it as a biologically confirmed
  species found throughout the Eastern Mediterranean and the Caucasus. It is on
  the Georgia checklist. A CaBOL barcode is from Chachuna Managed Reserve; a
  paper figure also shows Dalis Mta reservoir. Its worm-like body can be
  confused with the javelin sand boa (Eryx jaculus), but the build is different.
habitat: >-
  Loose soil and underground galleries. In Georgia a barcoded specimen is from
  Chachuna Managed Reserve; a figure locality is Dalis Mta reservoir.
diet: >-
  Ants, termites and their brood.
behavior: >-
  Fossorial; rarely seen on the surface.
conservation: >-
  Biologically confirmed in the checklist.
