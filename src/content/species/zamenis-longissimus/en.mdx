overview: >-
  The Aesculapian snake (Zamenis longissimus) is a harmless colubrid.
  Tarkhnishvili et al. 2026 treat it as a biologically confirmed species.
  Published phylogeography recognises several mitochondrial clades; an “Asian
  clade” is native to the eastern Black Sea area (Musilová et al. 2010). It is
  on the Georgia checklist. It is often confused with the Transcaucasian
  ratsnake (Zamenis hohenackeri).
