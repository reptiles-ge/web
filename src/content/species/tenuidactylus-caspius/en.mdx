overview: |-
  The Caspian Thick-toed Gecko (Tenuidactylus caspius) is the only gecko species confirmed in Georgia, and it is harmless to people. In the wild, it lives in the country's far southeast. One important site where it has been confirmed is Samukhi Valley in Dedoplistskaro Municipality. Specimens from Tbilisi have also been studied.
  Separate city populations are known as well. This gecko has been seen in towns of eastern Georgia, as well as in Kutaisi and Sukhumi. Its presence in cities does not mean that every small lizard on a wall is a gecko: often it may be a Caucasian Agama or another lizard. The gecko itself may also be mistaken for a Caucasian Agama when you see it on a wall. Vertical pupils and expanded toes will especially help you tell them apart.
