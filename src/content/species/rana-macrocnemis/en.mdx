overview: >-
  The Caucasian brown frog (Rana macrocnemis) is biologically confirmed.
  Ishchenko (1978) and Tarkhnishvili et al. (1999, 2001) accept two parapatric
  subspecies in Georgia: R. m. macrocnemis and R. m. camerani; their ranges do
  not match Veith et al. (2003). The taxonomic status of R. m. camerani needs
  population-genomic work and formal rank may be revised — it remains a listed
  species here, with subspecies mentioned only as the paper does. Barcodes:
  Tabatskuri (camerani) and Bjolieti, Guria (macrocnemis). The checklist figure
  is Guria, Bjolieti.
